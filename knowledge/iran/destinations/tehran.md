Tehran is the capital of Iran and one of the country's main cultural, economic, and transportation hubs.

The city combines historic royal sites, major national museums, contemporary art spaces, traditional bazaars, modern neighborhoods, cafes, restaurants, and direct access to the foothills of the Alborz Mountains.

Travelers may use Tehran as an arrival hub, a cultural destination, or a starting point for journeys to other parts of Iran.
